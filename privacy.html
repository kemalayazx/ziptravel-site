Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

This policy explains what the ZipTravel website and the ZipTravel Auto Poster tool do with data. The short version: the tool is private, it serves one person — the channel owner — and it collects no data from visitors or from anyone else.

1. Data we collect from visitors

None. This website is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising scripts, and does not track, profile or identify visitors.

2. Data the publishing tool handles

ZipTravel Auto Poster is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own accounts. To publish a video it handles:

  • Access tokens issued by the platform when the owner authorises the tool. They are stored privately on the owner's own server and used only to upload the owner's videos.
  • The owner's basic account information (such as the account name), returned by the platform during authorisation, used only to confirm the upload is going to the right account.
  • Videos and their captions that we produced ourselves and chose to publish.

3. What we never do

  • We do not collect, store or process any other user's personal data.
  • We do not read, scrape or analyse other people's videos, comments or profiles.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with advertisers, brokers or any third party.
  • We do not use platform data for training models or for any purpose beyond publishing our own videos.

4. Storage and security

Tokens and credentials are kept in restricted files on a private server, readable only by the owner's account. Nothing is stored in a public repository or shared service.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use and are deleted when the connection is no longer needed. The owner can revoke access at any time in the platform's own settings, which immediately invalidates the token.

6. Third-party platforms

Videos are published to platforms such as TikTok and YouTube. Once published, the content is also governed by those platforms' own privacy policies and terms.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no data about visitors or third parties, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or erase. If you believe otherwise, contact us and we will look into it.
