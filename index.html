What we make

ZipTravel is an independent creator channel publishing short vertical videos every day. Each one is a small window into a place as it actually is: a morning street, a local market, a roadside meal, a walk through a neighbourhood few visitors ever see.

🌏 Next stopsThe channel travels — new places are added as we go.

Publishing tools

We use our own small in-house tool, ZipTravel Auto Poster, to help publish the channel's own videos to our own social accounts. It prepares each upload with a title, description and hashtags, and sends the video to the account owner's own drafts for review before anything is published. It is used only for content we create ourselves, only on accounts we own, and it never accesses, collects or posts other people's content.
